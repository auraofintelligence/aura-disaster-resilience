# DAT-02 What information should each record carry so people can understand its source, timing, current meaning and later corrections?

## Why this matters

Good records keep a cancellation, a continuing warning and an uncertain update understandable even after a restart or lost connection.

## What to bring back

A record guide with worked warnings, service notices and calendar occurrences, covering identity, source, times, validity, whether the device clock can be trusted, replacements, corrections and continuing instructions.

Give each record a stable identity, source, issue and receipt times, applicable place, status and replacement links. Show information age and clock confidence plainly, and retain corrections so people can understand what changed.

### Existing-practice adaptation

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Adapt CAP's warning-record ideas where the source uses them: sender, identifier, sent time, status, update and cancellation references. Add separate local receipt and checking times without inventing an expiry or changing an issuer's instructions.
