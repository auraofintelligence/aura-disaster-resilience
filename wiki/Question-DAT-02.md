## Why this matters

Good records keep a cancellation, a continuing warning and an uncertain update understandable even after a restart or lost connection.

## What to bring back

A record guide with worked warnings, service notices and calendar occurrences, covering identity, source, times, validity, whether the device clock can be trusted, replacements, corrections and continuing instructions.
