# PHY-09 How will portable and replaceable field devices be carried, secured, watched, brought back and replaced as conditions change?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A clear setup and retrieval plan helps a field team put communication where it is needed and recover equipment safely.

## What to bring back

Prepare a packing and setup checklist with lifting limits, temporary anchors, weather and withdrawal triggers, and steps for lost contact, theft or damage. Practise retrieval and replacement while messages keep moving.
