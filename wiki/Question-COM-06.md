## Why this matters

Well-described observations give people evidence they can understand and check.

## What to bring back

Make a sensor register linking each measurement to its purpose, power use and reviewer. Include calibration - checking it against a known reference - maintenance, faults, privacy and human confirmation. Clearly separate measured data, estimates, derived results and simulated readings.

### Decision-led sensor register

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Use energy-controller readings and independently checked temperature/moisture sensors first. Compare a commercial asset tracker for portable modules, recognising that a tracker's onboard temperature may be unsuitable for precise environmental measurements.

Research basis: [Cerbo GX manual: configuration](https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/Cerbo_GX/en/configuration.html) · [Oyster3 GPS asset tracker](https://www.digitalmatter.com/devices/oyster3/).

### Cross-checked sensing twin

**AI-generated future concept.** Develop an Aura that compares redundant sensors, model predictions and human observations, then proposes a maintenance check when they diverge. Future robotic inspection could supply another labelled observation rather than silently turning a prediction into a fact.
