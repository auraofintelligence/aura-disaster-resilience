Recognise one verified elapsed hour of voluntary human contribution as one C-Hour. Each community chooses reciprocal recognition; keep records correctable, count time once across projects and maintain separate records for money, paid work, machine time and outcomes.

### Existing-practice adaptation

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Use a participant-confirmed task record with start and finish times, voluntary consent and a reviewer. Let the local community publish its own recognition opportunities, availability and review process. C-Hours have no monetary equivalent, sale or transfer.
