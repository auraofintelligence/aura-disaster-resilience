## Why this matters

Matching the message to the connection helps urgent notices get through while richer maps and simulations remain useful locally.

## What to bring back

Make a service-and-connection table for notices, maps, requests, device status and sensor readings. Show message size, acceptable delay, priority, sender authority, privacy and receipt status. Compare essential-service and richer-service options.
