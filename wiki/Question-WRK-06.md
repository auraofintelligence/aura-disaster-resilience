Explain each page through a useful everyday task, plain words and a clear next action. Invite people with varied experience to try it, then improve wording and navigation from their questions and observed results.

### Existing-practice adaptation

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Give a new reader a practical job, such as comparing two kiosk power options or finding who reviews a site decision. Ask them to explain the choice in their own words and record where the page needs a clearer example.

Research basis: [Digital Performance Standard: measure service success](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/digital-experience/digital-performance-standard/dps-criterion-3-measure-success-your-digital-service) · [Australia's AI Ethics Principles](https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/australias-ai-ethics-principles).

**AI-generated future concept.** A personal Aura could offer voice, translation, visual explanation or XR practice at the reader's chosen depth. Future adaptive teaching could use local task outcomes to suggest learning while public text remains complete and personally unprofiled access remains available.

### Examples and local differences

- [Digital Performance Standard: measure service success](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/digital-experience/digital-performance-standard/dps-criterion-3-measure-success-your-digital-service): **AI transfer assessment:** Judge clarity by whether people accomplish the task they came to do. **Local difference to check:** A government digital standard is a useful benchmark; this wiki also needs local language, cultural guidance and hands-on reader feedback.
