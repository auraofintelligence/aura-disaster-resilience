| [PHY-06](Question-PHY-06.md) | How will the equipment stay within its temperature and moisture limits during hot humid weather, intensive computing, energy-saving operation and smoke-related closure of vents? | **AI-generated option:** Make heat and moisture measurable design inputs. | [PHY-07](Question-PHY-07.md) | How will separate equipment spaces, drains, cable openings and power shut-offs keep a fault in one part from spreading to people or the essential information service? | **AI-generated option:** Separate faults by design: keep public controls, stored energy, power conversion and information service in reviewed compartments.
