## What to bring back

Prepare an after-action record linking the exercise or event ID, actual traces, crew and resident feedback, differences from the plan, proposed changes and responsible reviewer. Follow one failure through a revised instruction or design, a human decision and another check. Preserve the original record alongside the improvement.
