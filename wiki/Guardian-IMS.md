Think of the working desk behind a public notice: people record what happened, locate it on a map, assign work, report progress and hand over to the next shift. ## How people with Auras could connect

The following levels are **AI-generated design proposals**, to develop through agreed information and service interfaces. Aura of Intelligence is Luke's personal AI operating system in development, from light to ultra high definition. A person chooses what context to share; public information and assistance remain available through guest and assisted routes.

| Proposed level | Useful experience | Agreement or evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Find and understand public information** | An Aura helps someone find a relevant notice, hear or read an explanation, and follow the issuer's action. The original source, area, time and correction remain visible. | Approved public source or feed; reuse and accessibility conditions; clear separation of source content and AI explanation. |
| **Prepare and send a person's request** | With the person's choices, an Aura helps describe the need and sends the permitted information to an agreed receiving service. It shows locally saved, received and accepted as distinct states. | Supported submission route, minimum required information, receiving duty, consent or other applicable basis, receipts, escalation and correction. A personal profile need not be a condition of assistance. |
| **Assist an authorised worker** | A worker's Aura could prepare a draft task, retrieve an approved procedure, assemble a source-linked report or support a shift handover. | Actual staff identity, supported delegation and role permissions, review/publishing rules, audit trail and revocation. Personal use of an Aura does not create staff privileges. |
| **Coordinate continuing community services** | The proposed kiosk network connects permitted needs, offers, learning and resource readiness across everyday service and recovery. | Agreed ownership and referrals, qualified cover, current availability and a maintained exchange contract. Sensitive records retain their appropriate access. |
| **Grow AI/SI, twins, XR and robotics capability** | More capable assistants could compare dependencies, rehearse an event and prepare better options; supervised equipment could perform agreed inspection or delivery tasks. | Demonstrated capability and authorised scope for the specific task. Preserve observations, model assumptions, human decisions and results. A tool's greater intelligence does not itself appoint an operational decision-maker. |

One practical example is a cancelled ferry: an Aura explains the checked cancellation, helps a person state an accommodation or assistance need if they choose, and shows the receiving service's real response. A disconnected kiosk can preserve the request with a clear local status. The agreed service controls acceptance, allocation and response; a later connection reconciles the record and its receipts.

## A practical route to seeking a partnership

1. **Prepare a shared problem brief.** Use the [Council integration evidence template](../templates/council-integration-evidence.md): people served, existing arrangement, proposed Aura contribution, sample journey, information needed and measurable benefit. The existing [JOB-15](Progress.md) can carry this work.
2. **Seek Council's sponsorship for discovery.** Through the [Council approach route](Council-integration.md), ask the appropriate service owner to confirm Guardian's role, contract/interface permissions and relevant reviewers. Prepare the material now; actual outreach is a separate recorded action.
3. **Invite a joint technical and service discussion with QIT Plus.** Use its [published contact/demonstration route](https://www.qitplus.com/contact-copy/) when authorised. Ask which supported feed, export, API, webhook or commissioned connection fits the use case. A webhook is a message sent when something changes. Agree a suitable user-group or roadmap contribution route rather than assuming membership or access.
4. **Choose the partnership and support arrangement.** Identify the actual applicant/operator and parties, information custody, licences, intellectual property, accessibility, fees, liability/insurance, procurement requirements and ongoing maintenance. Keep ownership of a person's Aura context separate from an incident record's lawful custodianship.
5. **Build with fictional data in a test environment.** Agree the exchange format, identifiers, dates, permissions and receipts. Test a changed warning, duplicate request, withdrawn permission, interrupted connection, failed component and relief handover. Compare outcomes and staff effort with an agreed baseline.
6. **Review a bounded pilot and then extend it.** Record the exact site, service, configuration, support and responsible decisions. Demonstrated benefits can support broader integration while Council, QIT Plus and community participants continue improving their parts.

This pathway works under the user's scenario of Guardian continuing and evolving. It also encourages documented, portable exchanges so a person's Aura and the community service can remain useful as suppliers and software editions change.

## Questions for a supported connection

| Area | What to establish together |
| --- | --- |
| Interface and permissions | Which modules, fields and actions are available? What authentication, user delegation, test environment, change notifications, limits and compatibility promises apply? Public visibility, a user account and permission for an automated connection are distinct scopes. |
| Disconnected operation | Which functions work without which connection? Where are queued records held, how are they protected, and what confirms receipt after reconnection? Preserve issuer-supplied validity/expiry, review dates, corrections and unresolved conflicts. |
| Data and AI use | Who holds which records, for what purpose and duration? What can an Aura retain, retrieve or explain? Which model providers may process it, under what restrictions, and how are correction, withdrawal and training-data choices handled? |
| Security and hosting | Request applicable certification scope/current certificates, hosting and backup locations, access controls, incident procedures, independent assurance and restoration evidence for the chosen service. QIT's site states ISO 27001/9001 certification; certificate-level and deployment coverage were not independently established here. [Vendor statement](https://www.qitplus.com/) |
| Privacy and commercial support | QIT's public privacy policy displays **07/01/20** and covers website/business information. Establish the current customer data arrangements and service/support agreement. A public policy, app-store declaration or marketing description cannot specify every Council deployment. [Privacy policy](https://www.qitplus.com/privacy-policy/) |
| Shared success and continuity | Who maintains the connector, explains a failed exchange and pays continuing costs? Which measures show better comprehension, correct handovers, less duplicate entry and useful outage performance? How are records exported and responsibilities handed over if the connection ends? |
