# TWIN-01 What dated local digital twin—a computer representation of a person, place or system—will support useful kiosk navigation and outage planning?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Measured places, photographs, catalogue entries and inferred map features each carry different evidence. People need to see that difference when planning a journey or service.

## What to bring back

Define the place record: source, date, position and accuracy, measured or inferred features, access permissions, gaps and correction owner. Review representative routes and facilities. Keep uncertain points visible as uncertain, and distinguish a candidate gathering place from an officially activated shelter.
