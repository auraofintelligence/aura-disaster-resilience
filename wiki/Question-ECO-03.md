## Why this matters

Transport connects residents, visitors, supplies and relief crews. Useful advice identifies who issued it, when it applies and the alternatives actually confirmed.

## What to bring back

Agree the transport information record: issuing authority, affected route or service, check time, start and expiry, closures, access needs and uncertainty. Rehearse a suspended ferry, changed last return, offline kiosk and a situation awaiting a confirmed alternative. Keep planning maps distinct from current travel instructions.
