## Why this matters

A useful resilience plan follows the whole service: the message, the person responding, the equipment they need and the resources that keep it going.

## What to bring back

Draw these dependencies and replay failures involving staff and skills, charging, fuel, relevant cooling, freight, spare parts, waiting requests and transport access. At each change, identify the service retained, the adjustment needed and who takes responsibility. Link the exercise to the current evidence and resource assumptions.
