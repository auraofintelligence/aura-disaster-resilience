# SW-03 How will an approved update reliably replace an older notice, including when a kiosk is offline or an update stops halfway?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A ferry cancellation or safety update needs a clear source, current status and complete replacement of earlier advice.

## What to bring back

Prepare a versioned contents list showing the publisher's digital signature, approval scope, start time, expiry and items replaced. Demonstrate interrupted downloads, cancellation, repeated old messages, conflicting updates, an uncertain device clock and recovery. The screen should show a complete identified edition throughout.
