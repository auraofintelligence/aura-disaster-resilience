# The first choices to work through together

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

These three packages give parallel work a common starting point. Each offers alternatives, a recommended next step and a defined review scope. We can prepare research across the stack while people consider these choices.

## [D-001 Work together through linked questions and human decisions](Decision-D-001.md)

Choose how this wiki becomes our shared workshop: questions name the work, AI assistants prepare evidence and options, and people decide the next step.

**Recommended:** Use the linked wiki as the reading and question map, GitHub issues for review conversations, and versioned repository records for evidence and history.

## [D-002 Design a kiosk family around its weather and working conditions](Decision-D-002.md)

Choose a design approach that makes the public equipment, its power and its maintenance fit the conditions it will face. The existing artwork starts the visual conversation; this package starts the physical design.

**Recommended:** Develop coordinated designs for a sheltered arcade demonstrator, an operational outdoor public kiosk and a portable continuity kit. Compare freestanding and host-supported arrangements before choosing the outdoor build.

## [D-003 Build a complete everyday-to-disaster service demonstration](Decision-D-003.md)

Choose the first connected jobs to demonstrate, so we can test the kiosk, information, people, messages and Aura learning as a working whole.

**Recommended:** Start with a connected service demonstration using sample records at three logical locations and a portable unit. Practise everyday work, lost connections and recovery, then prepare the measured hardware and field trials.
