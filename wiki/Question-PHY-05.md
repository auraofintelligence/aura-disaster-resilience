## Why this matters

Specific conditions give everyone a shared meaning for all-weather performance and a fair way to test it.

## What to bring back

Make a test table for wind, driven rain, dust, salt, heat, smoke, flooding, flying debris, damage and fire exposure. State strength, direction and duration where evidence supports them, test preparation, what counts as passing, who reviews it, and the conditions still to test. Separate everyday weather, the chosen design event and more extreme events.
