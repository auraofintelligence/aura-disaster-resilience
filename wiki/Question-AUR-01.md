# AUR-01 What useful navigation can a first-time visitor receive from a personal Aura, the proposed AI assistant, while choosing how much personal context to share?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Residents and visitors should be able to reach essential help as guests, then add remembered preferences or other personal context when it helps them.

## What to bring back

Walk through an everyday visit and a disaster visit with optional personal details, source-linked answers, clear local availability and an understandable exit. Demonstrate essential information access for a guest and a returning Aura user. Keep the separate Genesis programme a voluntary further path.
