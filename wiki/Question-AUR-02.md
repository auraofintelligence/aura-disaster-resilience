## Why this matters

The intended journey concerns personal understanding and capability. Screen pixels, where the work runs and emergency energy settings are separate choices.

## What to bring back

Describe each proposed level's personal context, permitted tasks, evidence quality and useful outcomes. Measure answer quality, uncertainty, privacy, response time and energy on shared examples. Show computing location and display choices separately. Keep the deeper Genesis twin programme opt-in and label these levels as proposals awaiting evaluation.
