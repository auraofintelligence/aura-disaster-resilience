# SW-06 How will a person's request for supplies or assistance reach a responsible relief operator and show what has actually happened?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Sending a request, a person accepting it and help arriving are distinct steps. Clear progress helps residents and crews decide what to do next.

## What to bring back

Draw the request's journey from draft to delivery, acknowledgement, acceptance, handover and closure. Set out privacy, consent, repeated requests, expiry, withdrawal and escalation. Demonstrate a power cut, a duplicate request, an unavailable recipient and an urgent request still awaiting acknowledgement.

Create a private request with an understandable receipt and separate milestones for saved, sent, received, accepted, handed over and closed. The agreed receiving service would own acceptance and escalation. A waiting request would explain current contact options and its last confirmed status.

### Offline collection plus a receiving-service workflow

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Trial ODK for a structured, consent-based non-emergency request. Add the operator's acceptance and fulfilment records separately, so a server upload cannot be presented as help being assigned or on its way.

Research basis: [ODK Collect: managing draft, ready-to-send and sent forms](https://docs.getodk.org/collect-forms/) · [RFC 9171: Bundle Protocol Version 7](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9171.html).

### An Aura checks progress across authorised handovers

**AI-generated future concept.** Explore a personal Aura tracking an approved request's receipts and preparing a reminder or alternate contact proposal. People would retain the choice to disclose more information or approve a changed recipient.
