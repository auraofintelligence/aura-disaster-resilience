# AUR-06 How will a person decide which actions their Aura may read about, draft, propose or carry out on their behalf?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

An assistant becomes more useful when its permissions are understandable and the person can see, approve, stop and trace an action.

## What to bring back

Prepare an action-permission table with a preview, exact information used, responsible person, expiry, cancellation and completion record. Rehearse a request waiting offline until its permission expires, a repeated action and a public notice still awaiting release approval. Carry existing user authorisation forward within its scope.
