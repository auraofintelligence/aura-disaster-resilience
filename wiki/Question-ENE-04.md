## Why this matters

An electrical design gives qualified installers and local maintainers clear instructions for a public installation.

## What to bring back

Prepare an electrical connection diagram, wiring drawings and cable routes. Have qualified reviewers explain earthing and protection against faults, lightning and surges - sudden rises in voltage. Show isolation points, where power is switched off safely before work. Map each installation, inspection and repair task to the qualification needed.
