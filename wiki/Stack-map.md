The disaster resilience network brings everyday usefulness and preparedness together. A tradesperson could follow an approved equipment checklist, a driver find confirmed transport information, an artist publish an accessible event, a nurse find a current service contact, and a student or retiree learn through a practical community exercise. Personal AI, shared information, reliable equipment and people who know what to do would support those journeys together.

This is the planning stage for the proposed service. Source documents, code demonstrations and public workbenches provide foundations. The next milestones are a shared service brief, reviewable demonstrations, community discussion, agreed responsibilities, measured performance and supervised field use.

The [ecosystem register](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-disaster-resilience/blob/main/data/ecosystem.json) maps the related projects and source repositories to the questions they help answer. It also retains relevant public website and library links. The [source library](Source-library.md) preserves the supplied planning originals and project editions; private personal and cultural records retain their chosen access conditions.

## Three kinds of connection

- **Core:** a responsibility or design commitment the service will develop, such as useful public information, personal guidance, accountable operation and continuity.
- **Interface:** an agreed way for projects or services to exchange permitted information or support. Each connection will describe what happens when it is available, delayed or unavailable.
- **Context:** values, research, public participation, storytelling and future possibilities that help people ask better questions and evaluate the work.

A source repository offers material to learn from. The service team will choose which patterns to adapt and demonstrate. Each connected initiative can progress through its own people, evidence, resources and agreements while contributing to the shared direction.

A digital twin is a versioned representation of a person, place or system, built from selected evidence and assumptions. Maps, photographs, observations and simulations will retain their dates and different meanings. People will be able to inspect a claim, propose a correction and understand what remains uncertain. A personal twin develops through that person's choices about what it represents, who can use it and how it is updated.

Legal RAG, or retrieval-augmented generation, is a pattern where AI finds relevant source passages before preparing an explanation. The current legal explorations are [Your Relevance](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/australian-law-2012-lukes-relevance/pages/your-relevance.html), [P4A Legal RAG](https://p4a.xyz/pages/legal-rag.html) and [Australian Legal Engine](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/australian-legal-engine). These help us explore source-led understanding, precise retrieval and people making informed choices. The service would develop its collection of official instructions and approved procedures with the responsible information owners. A source document informs the work; actual people and organisations decide matters within their authority.

## Values, people and personal choice

**GAJRA Earth** means **Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth**. Joy, responsibility and abundance are the values starting point, considered together and open to local interpretation and revision. Public information and assistance remain available to everyone. Pledges, association membership, personal AI profiles and research participation are separate choices.

**Ready S.E.T. Co-op**, fully **Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative**, is the proposed intermediary for kiosks, the Cultural Intelligence Node and the Screen and Media Network. Community discussion, formation, membership, host arrangements, staffing and continuing resources are the milestones for developing that role. Relevant cultural custodians will determine how their material and participation are handled.

Aura of Intelligence is the personal AI operating system [Luke Nathan Hayes](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/open-record-luke-nathan-hayes/tree/main) is designing from light to ultra high definition. A person would choose the depth of context and models they bring together. Personal context, screen detail, computing location and emergency energy settings are independent choices. When a demanding view or AI service is unavailable, locally stored approved information and useful human contact routes would remain available.

C-Hours offer voluntary contribution recognition. Each community chooses its reciprocal rewards. One verified elapsed hour has no monetary equivalent and cannot be sold or transferred. Paid work, money, machine time and outcomes remain separate, with a person's elapsed time counted once across linked projects.

## Build connections people can rely on

Start with the [service envelope](Question-OPS-01.md), meaning the people, tasks and conditions the first complete version will support. For each task, trace the information, responsible people, equipment, power, communications and access it needs. This makes both the everyday journey and the disaster fallback reviewable.

A local release is a complete approved edition of the information and tools stored on a device. It would include essential text, maps and learning material so the kiosk can start and remain useful without outside hosting. The [communications work](Domain-COM.md) will show which information can travel, how long it may take and what people should see when a connection changes.

The Cultural Intelligence Node offers a pathway to serious community-controlled computing for learning, culture and shared work. Purpose will guide where each task runs: on a personal device, nearby equipment, a community node or a larger service. The kiosk continuity unit would keep essential local functions available while richer computing grows around it.

Every interface will have an information-sharing agreement: owner, permitted records, version, timestamps, uncertainty, permissions, capacity, outage behaviour and correction path. Food availability would come with condition and custodian information. Transport availability would come from its operator. A proposed energy source would become part of readiness after its actual connection and performance are demonstrated.

## Progress through evidence and human decisions

1. **Define the service together.** Prepare the service brief, source register, choices and evidence gaps. Agents can complete defined research, design and checking tasks, with a clear output for human review.
2. **Demonstrate connected journeys.** Use labelled sample records to join notices, event changes, help requests, personal AI guidance and practical learning. Rehearse both normal use and interruptions.
3. **Measure demanding conditions.** Agree the [test envelope](Question-TEST-01.md), the users, devices, demand and failures to test. Check what reaches the actual display, bring disconnected records back together and practise recovery. Record simulation, bench measurement and site demonstration as distinct achievements.
4. **Prepare supervised field use.** Bring site, equipment, people, information, cultural conditions and support into the [pilot readiness record](Question-TEST-05.md). Luke drives the proposals as project initiator. Where they overlap professional responsibilities, relevant reviewers compare his research with applicable good practice and legal sources, recording differences, improvements and deficiencies. Actual hosts, custodians, receiving services, statutory authorities and qualified practitioners decide the matters within their responsibilities. [Professional review](Professional-review.md) connects that work.
5. **Grow demonstrated capability.** Use [review criteria](Question-TEST-06.md) to learn from outcomes, improve the service, add useful connections and match growth to trained people and continuing resources.

The question pages track evidence, proposed answers, progress and decisions. Current work will turn source access, host availability, staffed help, qualified cover, measured power, network geography and continuing budgets into specific arrangements that people can understand and rely on.
