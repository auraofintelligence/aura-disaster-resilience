# AUR-03 How will the personal Aura operating system help someone find the next useful action as everyday services, transport and warning conditions change?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

People need guidance that remembers their purpose and explains the current local situation, with an easy route to further detail.

## What to bring back

Show a brief action card, an optional explanation and links to the evidence. Rehearse a change into disaster service, older sources, conflicting advice, uncertain information and different language or access needs. Include a person who chooses guest assistance and a person using their own Aura.
