# AUR-03 How will the personal Aura operating system help someone find the next useful action as everyday services, transport and warning conditions change?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

People need guidance that remembers their purpose and explains the current local situation, with an easy route to further detail.

## What to bring back

Show a brief action card, an optional explanation and links to the evidence. Rehearse a change into disaster service, older sources, conflicting advice, uncertain information and different language or access needs. Include a person who chooses guest assistance and a person using their own Aura.

Make an action card stating what the person can do, the issuing source and check time, and any confirmation still needed. The Aura could explain the card and compare options using chosen context. Official instructions and service availability would retain their actual source and status.

### Action cards from checked transport and warning records

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Combine current approved warning records with verified transport information, keeping schedules, predictions and confirmed closures distinct. Provide the same card as readable text and accessible audio, with a route to a responsible person.

Research basis: [Common Alerting Protocol Version 1.2](https://docs.oasis-open.org/emergency/cap/v1.2/CAP-v1.2-os.html) · [Translink open data: schedules and real-time feeds](https://translink.com.au/about-translink/open-data) · [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

### An Aura rehearses a changing plan with its user

**AI-generated future concept.** Explore a personal assistant comparing a ferry suspension or service closure against the person's chosen needs, then preparing a revised plan for confirmation. Show assumptions and withhold any claim that an unconfirmed alternative is available.

Research basis: [NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0: core functions](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) · [Translink open data: schedules and real-time feeds](https://translink.com.au/about-translink/open-data).
