# ENE-10 What checks, drawings and training records will accompany each power installation and later equipment change?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A clear handover helps the next installer, operator and maintainer understand the system and keep it ready.

## What to bring back

Prepare the commissioning pack - the records from checking an installation before use. Include its exact parts and settings, wiring, protection tests, energy reserve, differences from the reviewed design, maintenance owner, replacement triggers and tests to repeat after changes. Record the human review.
