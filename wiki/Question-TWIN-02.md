# TWIN-02 Which demanding exercises will show how the network serves people through combined, prolonged failures?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Practising the interaction of storms, fire, power loss, unavailable staff, ferry disruption and depleted supplies can reveal the improvements that matter most.

## What to bring back

Build a dated exercise set with starting conditions, timed events, resource assumptions, roles, expected service, observations and pause points. Prioritise common local hazards and include longer-duration dependency failures. Record wider horizon scenarios as assumptions for learning, with the service requirements from OPS-01 kept central.
