## Why this matters

Practising the interaction of storms, fire, power loss, unavailable staff, ferry disruption and depleted supplies can reveal the improvements that matter most.

## What to bring back

Prioritise common local hazards and include longer-duration dependency failures. Record wider horizon scenarios as assumptions for learning, with the service requirements from OPS-01 kept central.
