## Why this matters

Useful runtime depends on the actual work and equipment. Screen detail, AI functions and available energy each need their own measurements.

## What to bring back

Prepare an equipment comparison and repeatable practical checks using representative notices, requests, text, touch and voice. Measure start-up, response, power use, storage and sustained outage operation. Include uncertain time and low storage, and show the functions each arrangement retains as resources change.
