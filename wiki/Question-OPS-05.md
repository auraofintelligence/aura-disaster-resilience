# OPS-05 Which help requests could an agreed receiving service handle, and how should responsibility continue if a person or connection becomes unavailable?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A person asking for help should understand whether their message is saved, received, accepted or being acted on, and what to do next.

## What to bring back

A help-request agreement covering supported needs, immediate-danger routes, the meaning of each acknowledgement, responsibility, escalation, reassignment, review times and unavailable-service messages.
