# COM-10 What field exercises will show that messages reach the right places, at useful speeds, and recover after several things fail together?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Shared exercises turn network drawings into evidence that operators and residents can learn from.

## What to bring back

Prepare tests for busy and quiet traffic, a link that can send but cannot receive, failed hubs and relays, disconnected settlements, satellite loss, low power, damaged portable equipment and conflicting reports. Reconnect the network, record delay, capacity, priorities, recovery, limits and uncertainty, and invite human review.

### Measured mixed-link exercise

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Use agreed sample notices and requests across local Wi-Fi, candidate radio and ordinary or satellite uplinks. Record send, receive and acceptance times, backlog recovery, energy and weather; keep all public alerts clearly marked as exercise material.
