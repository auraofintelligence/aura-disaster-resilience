# ENE-05 When should the kiosk change its power use, and how will it keep essential information available as battery charge and supply change?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A clear operating sequence can preserve reserve while keeping useful services available.

## What to bring back

Write a step-by-step operating plan for each power state, including manual controls, automatic fault checks, logs and restart. Set different switch-on and switch-off points to prevent rapid cycling. Compare reserve levels and service priorities under the same tests.
