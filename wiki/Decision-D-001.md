## The choices

| Choice | What it brings | What to plan for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One long planning document | A single reading path explains the broad idea. | Assignments, changes and connected choices need extra tracking. |
| Linked question cards and review packages | Each job has an owner, evidence, connected questions and a review route. | Keep the records and published wiki in step as work changes. |
| Issues as the primary workspace | Discussion and assignments sit directly in GitHub. | Provide a separate reading map so newcomers can follow the whole system. |

## What people review

- Can a newcomer find a useful job and understand what to bring back?
- Can a reviewer see the exact proposal, evidence, responsibilities and next action?
- Do changed proposals return to review while earlier decisions remain traceable?
- Does the proposal identify overlapping professional responsibilities, applicable practice and legal sources, and the comparison of differences, improvements and deficiencies?
- Does the proposed councils, teams and committees process support near-future XR, personal Auras, AI/SI and robotics while keeping actual mandates, decisions and delegated tasks traceable?
