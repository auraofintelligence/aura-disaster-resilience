## The choices

| Choice | What it brings | What to plan for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One long planning document | A single reading path explains the broad idea. | Assignments, changes and connected choices need extra tracking. |
| Linked question cards and review packages | Each job has an owner, evidence, connected questions and a review route. | Keep the records and published wiki in step as work changes. |
| Issues as the primary workspace | Discussion and assignments sit directly in GitHub. | Provide a separate reading map so newcomers can follow the whole system. |
