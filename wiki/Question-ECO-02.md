# ECO-02 How will community media, stories and notices reach the right screens while urgent service messages take priority?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Everyday creative work can make the network valuable and familiar. Clear permissions and publishing responsibilities support that trust during a disaster.

## What to bring back

Define the release record: rights holder, permitted audience and devices, cultural consent, source edition, captions or other access formats, expiry, withdrawal and urgent-message priority. Check an interrupted programme, withdrawn image permission and saved offline copies, with private or unpublished work kept in its agreed workspace.
