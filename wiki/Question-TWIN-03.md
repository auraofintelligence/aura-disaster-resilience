# TWIN-03 What checks will show when a computer model is useful for a real decision and where its limits remain?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

People can learn more from a model when they can inspect its inputs, compare its results with observations and understand the uncertainty.

## What to bring back

Create a model and run record naming its purpose, source editions, assumptions, settings, uncertainty, errors and permitted uses. Compare an understandable baseline with the proposed model on cases reserved for evaluation. Publish what worked, what failed and the conditions still to investigate.
