| [FIN-01](Question-FIN-01.md) | What would the whole first service cost to establish, support and replace, and how well is each estimate evidenced? | **AI-generated option:** Build a dated whole-service cost inventory, including equipment, delivery, installation, staff, connectivity, content, training, insurance, maintenance and replacement. Compare options over an agreed planning period, with quotes, assumptions and the responsible payer visible.<br><br>**AI-generated future concept:** An Aura-assisted cost twin could connect measured energy use, faults, staff effort and parts consumption to the budget. Future AI/SI could propose savings or shared purchasing while people approve commitments and verify that service quality is retained.<br><br>**Research examples:** [Shoalhaven: Community Information Hubs Launched](https://getinvolved.shoalhaven.nsw.gov.au/rrp/news_feed/information-hubs) · [Digital Performance Standard: measure service success](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/digital-experience/digital-performance-standard/dps-criterion-3-measure-success-your-digital-service) | open |
| [FIN-02](Question-FIN-02.md) | What continuing income, operating budget and replacement reserves would keep the agreed service dependable? | **AI-generated option:** Plan continuing service with a funded operating budget and explicit replacement reserves. Compare membership support, service agreements, donations, earned work and grants as distinct possibilities, keeping confirmed income separate from prospective sources and access commitments.<br><br>**AI-generated future concept:** Use an Aura-supported budget dashboard to forecast replenishment and maintenance from actual service use. Future automation could reduce routine administration, with measured savings and human review before changing staffing, public access or reserve contributions.<br><br>**Research examples:** [Strengthening Rural Communities: Prepare & Recover](https://frrr.org.au/funding/src-prepare-recover/) · [Digital Performance Standard: measure service success](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/digital-experience/digital-performance-standard/dps-criterion-3-measure-success-your-digital-service) | open |
| [FIN-03](Question-FIN-03.md) | Which funding opportunities fit particular parts of the service, and what evidence would an applicant or supporting organisation need? | **AI-generated option:** Match separate work packages to verified funding rules and actual applicant readiness. Explore Redland community funding and FRRR resilience funding, resolve equipment eligibility questions and confirm any auspice, public benefit and co-funding arrangements before relying on them.<br><br>**AI-generated future concept:** Aura-assisted grant research could flag changed rules and assemble a source-linked draft from approved evidence. A shared commitments register could prevent duplicate funding of the same cost while people check the application, relationships and reporting obligations.<br><br>**Research examples:** [Redland City Council: Community Support Grant](https://www.redland.qld.gov.au/Grants-and-sponsorship/Community-Support-Grant) · [Strengthening Rural Communities: Prepare & Recover](https://frrr.org.au/funding/src-prepare-recover/) | open |
