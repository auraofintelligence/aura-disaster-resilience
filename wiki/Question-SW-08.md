# SW-08 How will the island's kiosks and portable equipment stay connected through the routes available at each proposed site?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Radio reach, a local relay and an internet connection have different strengths and failure points. Field evidence will show which combination supports the service.

## What to bring back

Draw candidate device roles and message routes. Prepare site checks for reach, connection quality, heavy traffic, separated areas, loss of an internet connection point and a portable relay. Show which service functions work on each route and measure recovery against OPS-01.
