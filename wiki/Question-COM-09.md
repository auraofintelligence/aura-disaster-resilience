# COM-09 What parts, saved settings, security access, tools and trained people will let us replace a device or repair a connection while the island is isolated?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A prepared repair team can restore service even when the usual expert, an online account or a ferry delivery is unavailable.

## What to bring back

Create illustrated repair and replacement instructions, protected copies of settings, a spares list with check dates, safe limits for antenna and radio work, and a drill with the usual maintainer absent. Keep the skills needed for each step visible.
