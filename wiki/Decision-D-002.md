# D-002 Design a kiosk family around its weather and working conditions

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

**Review scope:** The physical design brief and comparison drawings. **State:** ready for people to review

Choose a design approach that makes the public equipment, its power and its maintenance fit the conditions it will face. The existing artwork starts the visual conversation; this package starts the physical design.

## The choices

| Choice | What it brings | What to plan for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A freestanding outdoor kiosk | A visible public point can be placed where people gather and need information. | Resolve enclosure, heat, water, wind, anchoring, safe access and maintenance together. |
| A public interface supported by a weather-protected host | The public screen connects to computing and service equipment in an agreed protected space. | Agree access, host responsibilities, cabling, independent power and support when the host is closed. |
| A portable continuity kit | Crews can reach events, exercises and temporary gaps with a transportable unit. | Plan transport, safe setup, deployment conditions, storage and return. |

## Recommended next step

Develop coordinated designs for a sheltered arcade demonstrator, an operational outdoor public kiosk and a portable continuity kit. Compare freestanding and host-supported arrangements before choosing the outdoor build.

## What this decision moves forward

- Write the exposure and service requirements for each form.
- Prepare alternative layouts, compartments, access arrangements and a measured parts-and-energy plan.

## Later milestones with their own reviews

- Engineering review, prices and a build decision follow the comparison drawings.
- A real site brings its own host, cultural, installation and operating agreements.
- Measured weather, energy and communication performance supports a later field trial.

## What people review

- Are operating conditions, protection through a severe event, storage and recovery conditions clearly described?
- Can a person use and maintain the equipment comfortably and safely?
- Do the drawings show water paths, heat management, power separation, access and mounting?
- Does each claimed capability have a planned measurement or review?
