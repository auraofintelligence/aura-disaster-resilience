# COM-02 How should kiosks and devices that pass messages between them connect so each settlement can still receive useful information when a link or major device fails?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Terrain, trees, storms and damage can split a network into disconnected groups; planning alternate paths helps people stay connected.

## What to bring back

Draw two network layouts, called topologies. Show links that have been measured and those still assumed, how much they can carry, shared failure points and isolated settlements. Compare relays - devices that pass messages onward - and physical transfer by a person carrying data. State which services continue in each case.
