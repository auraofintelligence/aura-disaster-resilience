### Existing-practice adaptation

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Use a named review comment and its proposal fingerprint, with separate scopes for design direction, site access and professional assessment. Keep the attributable source record and review chronology so an earlier approval cannot silently override a later change request.
