Give each site a closure and continuity plan with a named decision role, last-confirmed availability and alternate public doorway. Share closure notices through remaining channels and explain uncertainty before directing people toward a replacement location.

### Existing-practice adaptation

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Adapt Wellington's alternate-venue approach into a local closure checklist: assess the site, stop unsuitable use, record time and reason, update other locations and arrange a portable service at a confirmed host. Keep printed directions available.

Research basis: [Wellington Region: Community Emergency Hubs](https://www.wremo.nz/get-ready/community-ready/community-emergency-hubs) · [Redland City Council: disasters and emergencies](https://www.redland.qld.gov.au/Community-safety-and-health/Disasters-and-emergencies).

### Future concept to explore

**AI-generated future concept.** Auras could compare signed availability reports and explain which options remain, with last-check times. A future portable robot or vehicle-based kiosk could relocate only after a competent person confirms the destination, route and operating scope.

### Examples and local differences

- [Wellington Region: Community Emergency Hubs](https://www.wremo.nz/get-ready/community-ready/community-emergency-hubs): **AI transfer assessment:** Prepare an alternate venue before the usual community doorway becomes unavailable. **Local difference to check:** A New Zealand hub guide cannot determine Queensland evacuation directions or prove a proposed alternate site is safe and open.
