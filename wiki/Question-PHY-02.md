## Why this matters

Local knowledge and site measurements help the kiosk keep serving people when roads, ferries or normal access change.

## What to bring back

Prepare a dated site sheet and map for each option. Mark what was observed, estimated or still needs checking; compare sheltered and exposed positions, identify the site owner, and show public and maintenance access.
