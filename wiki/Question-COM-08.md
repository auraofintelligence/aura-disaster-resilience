# COM-08 How will people and maintainers see whether a kiosk, connection, sensor, notice or request is current, working, waiting or uncertain?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Visible status helps people act on information and helps maintainers find the next repair.

## What to bring back

Design a public status display and a maintenance view. Show last contact, age of waiting messages and source information, uncertain time and battery state. Set local alerts and response steps, and keep private information out of unnecessary logs.
