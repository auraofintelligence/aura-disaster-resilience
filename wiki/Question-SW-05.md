# SW-05 How will shared equipment keep public information, private assistance, staff work and a person's optional Aura separate?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A resident should be able to get private help and leave the kiosk ready for the next person, with their information still under the agreed protection.

## What to bring back

Describe the separate workspaces, what each saves and how a session ends. Check screen changes, copied text, downloads, browsing history, stored records and AI context. Demonstrate that the next visitor and public display receive the intended public material while private details remain protected.
