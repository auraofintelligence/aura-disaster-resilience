# Rights and discovery

This project shares a growing body of disaster-kiosk designs, research, source documents and practical learning. People can read it, understand how the ideas developed and build on the work within the permissions below.

The [Strange But True Public Source Licence](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-disaster-resilience/blob/main/LICENCE.md) is the full statement of rights. Its established terms have been carried across with this project's name and address.

## Read, learn and give credit

The licence allows viewing, sharing, cloning, study, reference and adaptation for personal, educational, artistic, research, community and other non-commercial use. It also allows a personal AI assistant to help with those permitted uses.

For example, a resident can study a kiosk drawing, a student can explore an idea with a personal AI assistant, and someone can prepare an attributed non-commercial community explanation. Keep the original project and licence visible when using or adapting the work.

The licence sets a separate written-permission requirement for commercial, corporate, institutional, government, startup, agency, client and employer uses. That includes uses in paid products, consulting, courses, funded commercial projects and commercial AI systems. Ask Luke Nathan Hayes about the particular use and record the written agreement. The full licence sets the terms and takes precedence over this short guide.

People own the personal notes and other outputs they create where the licence describes that right. They remain responsible for what they include and share.

## Keep each source's rights attached

The project licence covers original work by Luke Nathan Hayes / Strange But True / Aura of Intelligence. A third-party document, photograph, library, font, platform or other source keeps its own terms.

For each archived source, keep its author or publisher, original address, date or edition, permission or licence, and any conditions on reuse with the file. A saved copy helps people check an argument and continue the work; inclusion in the library does not give that copy new rights.

Keep quotations, adaptations and original material identifiable. A public plan, research source and personal contribution can sit together while their ownership and permitted uses remain clear.

Public source-library and archive paths remain available for discovery under this pattern. Rights and attribution travel with those files.

## Where the robots policy applies

A crawler normally reads the robots file at the root of the host it visits, such as `https://auraofintelligence.github.io/robots.txt`.

A project file served at `/aura-disaster-resilience/robots.txt` expresses this project's policy but is below that host root. Its presence does not establish that the host's active policy has changed.

The [GitHub Pages root snippet](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-disaster-resilience/blob/main/.github/robots-root-snippet.txt) provides rules scoped to `/aura-disaster-resilience/` for an authorised host maintainer to merge with the existing root policy. The maintainer preserves other projects' rules and checks the published result. Preparing this snippet changes no other repository or host policy.

The native [GitHub Wiki](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-disaster-resilience/wiki) is served by GitHub. GitHub controls that host's robots policy. A file in this repository or a wiki page cannot replace it.

These distinctions let us state the intended policy clearly and record what has actually been published.
