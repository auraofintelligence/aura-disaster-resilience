# SW-09 How can a trained relief operator restore, replace or update a kiosk while the usual maintainer, internet and freight are unavailable?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A clear recovery pack and practised handover help local people keep the service useful through a long outage.

## What to bring back

Prepare offline instructions with labelled photos, a known-working software copy, checked updates, compatible spares and saved settings. Rehearse restoring records, returning to the earlier software edition and handing over to another operator. Record the relief operator's time, difficulties and support still needed.
