# D-003 Build a complete everyday-to-disaster service demonstration

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

**Review scope:** The first software and service demonstration. **State:** ready for people to review

Choose the first connected jobs to demonstrate, so we can test the kiosk, information, people, messages and Aura learning as a working whole.

## The choices

| Choice | What it brings | What to plan for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public information and calendar first | Demonstrate reviewed notices, events, corrections and saved information. | Request handovers and personal learning join in a later demonstration. |
| Connected service demonstration | Demonstrate public information, bounded assistance, readiness records and Aura learning across several simulated locations. | Use clear sample records and test the different receiving, approval and failure states. |
| Prepare a field pilot alongside the software | Develop a real host and hardware trial while the service takes shape. | Coordinate people, equipment, site arrangements and resources earlier. |

## Recommended next step

Start with a connected service demonstration using sample records at three logical locations and a portable unit. Practise everyday work, lost connections and recovery, then prepare the measured hardware and field trials.

## What this decision moves forward

- Demonstrate three everyday tasks: find checked events and transport information; find a local notice or service; use guided assistance or learning.
- Demonstrate disaster information, service/resource readiness and request handover with honest source and receipt states.
- Prepare a two-to-three-day practical service exercise with disconnected operation, staff handovers and recovery. Digital-twin simulations can cover any chosen time span, recorded separately from practical test duration and computer runtime.

## Later milestones with their own reviews

- People responsible for real information and requests agree their service arrangements before public use.
- Actual endurance, weather performance and pilot-site readiness are established through their own measurements and reviews.

## What people review

- Can residents and visitors complete the everyday jobs without a personal account?
- Does a changed or unresolved warning keep its source, time and meaning when a connection fails?
- Can people see whether a request is saved, received, assigned or completed?
- Does Aura offer relevant learning with a source, prerequisites and a human handover?
- Can another steward restore the service and continue outstanding work?
- Does the proposal identify overlapping professional responsibilities, applicable practice and legal sources, and the comparison of differences, improvements and deficiencies?
