# OPS-07 How should responsible people keep resource condition, availability, allocation and replenishment clear across disconnected locations?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Reliable resource information helps people use what is available and avoid promising the same stock or vehicle to two people.

## What to bring back

A resource workflow showing the custodian, usable and allocated quantities, condition checks, location access, review times, disputed records and replenishment needs.

Track each resource's custodian, location, usable quantity, reservations, condition and last check. Make allocations explicit, keep disputed or old counts visible and require physical confirmation before promising scarce supplies across disconnected locations.

### Existing-practice adaptation

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Use a needs-and-offers register with numbered reservations, custodians and a paper fallback. Reconcile counts at shift change and reconnection. Explore GIVIT's organisational request process for appropriate unmet needs through an agreed registered support organisation.

Research basis: [Wellington Region: Community Emergency Hubs](https://www.wremo.nz/get-ready/community-ready/community-emergency-hubs) · [GIVIT: national disaster resources](https://www.givit.org.au/disaster-resources/national-resources).

### Future concept to explore

**AI-generated future concept.** With consent, sensors or scanned labels could help custodians update stock and condition. An AI planner could suggest replenishment and a future delivery robot could execute a bounded task after allocation, route and handover are authorised.
