# ECO-06 How can repair, making, materials and training partnerships strengthen the kiosk's equipment and skills over time?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Local repair knowledge, useful spares and practised handovers can keep a service going while outside labour and freight are delayed.

## What to bring back

Define a repair-and-learning exchange covering equipment, fault description, safe first checks, parts, tools, qualified roles, local availability and task lessons. Include handover evidence. Show today's candidate support separately from longer-term materials, fabrication, tunnelling and storage research, so each connection has a clear next milestone.
