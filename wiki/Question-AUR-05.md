# AUR-05 How will a person's sharing choices travel with their records and AI work between a device, local hub and outside service?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

People should understand who can use their information, where it goes and how a change of service affects their choices.

## What to bring back

Set out the rules for original records, retrieved information, summaries, search aids and trained AI versions. Name purpose, destination, service operator, saved copies, expiry and exit arrangements. Check a denied transfer, changed permission, recovery and an attempted public answer using private material.

**AI-generated adaptation of an existing pattern.** Evaluate Solid-style selective access alongside an explicit record of exports, summaries and search indexes. Agree how each recipient handles expiry and deletion, and test the service provider's actual storage protections and recovery arrangements.

### Examples and local differences

- [Solid FAQ: personal data sharing, storage and copies](https://solidproject.org/faq): **AI transfer assessment:** Selective access helps control new sharing, while copies already exported need separate treatment. **Local difference to check:** The network must also govern offline copies, AI summaries, training permissions and cultural authority across different recipients.
