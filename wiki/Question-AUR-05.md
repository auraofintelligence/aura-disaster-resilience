# AUR-05 How will a person's sharing choices travel with their records and AI work between a device, local hub and outside service?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

People should understand who can use their information, where it goes and how a change of service affects their choices.

## What to bring back

Set out the rules for original records, retrieved information, summaries, search aids and trained AI versions. Name purpose, destination, service operator, saved copies, expiry and exit arrangements. Check a denied transfer, changed permission, recovery and an attempted public answer using private material.
