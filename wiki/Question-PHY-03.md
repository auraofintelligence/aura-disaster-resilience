# PHY-03 How will people of different heights, abilities and ages approach, reach, read, hear and use the kiosk in bright light, darkness and rain?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A well-placed screen and clear approach make the service useful to someone standing, using a wheelchair, carrying shopping or helping another person.

## What to bring back

Make full-size layouts with measurements, viewing angles, shelter and clear paths. Try them with standing, seated and assisted users. Include paper and audio options where appropriate, and record what people can complete.
