# AUR-04 How can just-in-time learning help a resident or relief operator learn the next permitted task and recognise when a qualified person is needed?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

Learning at the point of need can build confidence through explanation, practice, feedback and a clear understanding of responsibility.

## What to bring back

Prepare a reusable lesson for actual equipment: task, prerequisites, source edition, explanation, demonstration, practice, checks and pause or escalation points. Observe someone applying it, then trying a related task. Include an unfamiliar relief operator and record the support needed; keep instruction distinct from qualification and permission.
