# ECO-04 How will offers, supply information and requests connect residents with responsible relief operators while respecting household privacy?

[Start here](Home.md) · [Questions](Question-register.md) · [Progress](Progress.md) · [People decide](Human-review.md)

## Why this matters

A resource service becomes useful when everyone can distinguish an offer, an available item, an accepted request and a completed handover.

## What to bring back

Define an exchange record for the item or service, quantity, time, location-sharing scope, confidence in availability, responsible operator, consent and acknowledgement. Map prospective food, water, charging, equipment and assistance connections. Mark candidate partners as awaiting agreement and rehearse a request with each step's status and a documented handover.
