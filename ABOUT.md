# About Aura Disaster Resilience

Aura Disaster Resilience is a planning workshop for a community kiosk and resilience network on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island, also known as Straddie, on Redlands Coast in Queensland, Australia.

The proposed service connects everyday notices, events, assistance and learning with dependable power, communications, equipment readiness and local coordination through storms, cyclones, fires and longer disruptions. Its public wiki brings together design questions, source documents, possible answers, human decisions and practical checks.

[Luke Nathan Hayes](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/open-record-luke-nathan-hayes/tree/main) is the project initiator. **Aura of Intelligence** is the personal AI operating system he is designing, from light to ultra high definition. **Ready S.E.T. Co-op**, fully **Ready Sustainable Employment and Training Cooperative**, is the proposed operating intermediary. **GAJRA Earth**, the Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth, provides the values starting point.

Planning and design are underway. Community discussion, prototypes, measured trials and agreed operating arrangements are the next milestones. People who learn by doing can contribute alongside experienced technical and professional reviewers.
