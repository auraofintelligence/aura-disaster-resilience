    Start with people.
    Build capability together.

    Aura of Intelligence is Luke Nathan Hayes’s proposal for adaptive support throughout life. A personal Aura would help someone reflect, learn, organise chosen records and understand the next useful step. For disaster resilience, that relationship meets a practical community network: trusted information, local knowledge, learning, equipment and people who follow through.

    The starting place is Minjerribah, North Stradbroke Island, and Redlands. Community discussion, demonstrations and measured trials would shape a path through Queensland and Australia. The ambition is substantial; the public research, document and wiki give interested people a concrete place to begin. [P1]

    A demanding transition.
    A constructive bet.

    Smartphones already bring computing, cameras, microphones and location tools into daily life. An ACCC-commissioned survey found that 94% of Australians aged 14 and older had a personal smartphone in October-November 2024. This is a dated survey finding, rather than a claim about every household today. [R1]

    Artificial intelligence, or AI, adds ways to explain information and work with records. Superintelligence, or SI, describes possible capability beyond humans across many fields; its arrival is uncertain. Extended reality, or XR, connects digital material with real surroundings or immersive practice. Increasingly capable devices, sensors and robotics make personal agency and practical learning urgent concerns. [R2]

    Australia has less than six years before Brisbane 2032. The next season of disruption arrives much sooner. Aura proposes a Protopian gambit: a considered bet on improvements people test, compare and revise. Joyful Responsible Abundance joins worthwhile experiences, care for consequences and wider access to useful capability. A fair go includes people whose circumstances make preparation harder.

    The person remains the starting point

    The qualified self.

    A person is understood through their own explanations, values, abilities, relationships and experience. Measurements add observations; reflection gives them meaning. Onboarding records a minimum viable self, the first agreed representation from which a personal digital twin grows. People own their data and digital twins in Aura’s proposed model, with chosen services providing the business proposition. [P2]

    Useful before the storm.
    Useful long afterwards.

    Music, sport, culture, shared meals and practical work give a resilience network an everyday home. Familiar information points and people become easier to find when circumstances change. During disruption, the proposed service would preserve source meaning, show connection limits and keep requests traceable. Recovery continues through changing needs, memories, rest, work and renewed participation.

    Paid Try Everything opportunities, voluntary civic stewardship and community-shaped C-Hours offer different ways to contribute. Essential assistance follows people’s needs and actual service arrangements, independently of any contribution record.

    Where the work stands

    Available to examine

    The document, linked research, source archive, design questions, wiki and personal browser-tool foundations.

    Next to demonstrate

    A complete journey using sample people, notices, requests, learning tasks, interruptions and restoration.

    To develop with participants

    Hosts, protected personal continuity, measured equipment, service agreements, funding and supported trials.

    Publication of a plan establishes neither an operating emergency service nor an agency partnership. Early interest helps prioritise worthwhile work and develop shared targets and mutually acceptable terms.
