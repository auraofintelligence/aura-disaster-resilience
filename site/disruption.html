    Keep information and responsibility clear

    Explain the source without changing its meaning.

    The Australian Warning System uses three levels: Advice, Watch and Act, and Emergency Warning. The issuing organisation’s message and call to action carry the meaning. An Aura explanation would link back to that source and preserve its instructions, location and timing. [R6]

    A public display needs the issuer, issue time, last receipt and connection state. A stored record remains identifiable when outside links fail. Historical playback, a practice scenario and a current service update need unmistakably different presentation.

    This website explains the proposal. For current preparedness and warnings, use Get Ready Queensland and the issuing services it links. No live warning feed is operating on these pages.

    A request moves through real states.

    Writing a request is the beginning of a journey. The proposed service would show the state confirmed by the device and receiving organisation, making waiting work visible. A personal explanation also stays separate from the organisation’s accepted record. [P7]

    Proposed request states
    StateWhat has happened
    Saved locallyThe device holds the request; outside delivery is pending.
    DeliveredThe receiving system has recorded delivery.
    Accepted for reviewA receiving service has taken responsibility for review.
    AssignedThe responsible service has allocated a next action.
    Outcome recordedThe action and any remaining need are recorded.

    A resource offer also needs a custodian, condition, availability, suitable operator and return arrangements. A vehicle on a list is useful only within its actual readiness and permissions. Paid work, donated resources and voluntary time keep their different records.

    Illustrative exercise continued

    Anna’s message waits for a connection.

    The sample kiosk loses outside internet. Anna prepares a request about a support service. The device saves it locally and explains that delivery is waiting. After restoration, the receiving desk records delivery, then acceptance for review.

    The exercise deliberately introduces a duplicate. The team checks whether it creates two jobs, whether corrections reach both copies and whether Anna sees a consistent account. A relief steward also takes over, using the current information edition, equipment state and unresolved work.

    These are proposed test cases, with sample people and services. No actual request is being submitted or monitored by this website.

    A supported connection with existing services.

    Guardian IMS is QIT Plus incident-management software. Redland’s December 2025 Local Disaster Management Plan records its use in coordination and tasking. The project explores a supported Aura connection for public information, request preparation and authorised learning. Council and supplier participation, access and exchange arrangements remain to be developed. [R5] [R14] [P9]

    A personal Aura does not confer staff access or an agency role. Issuing notices, accepting requests and allocating resources remain decisions for the responsible organisations. The first useful comparison would follow a complete journey, including failure and restoration. [P8]
