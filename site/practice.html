    Place twins · Just-in-time learning · Playful teamwork

    A model people are able to question.

    A place twin is a dated digital representation built from selected evidence and assumptions. It might show an information point, an approach to a venue or a service relationship for an exercise. Its value depends on what it represents accurately and what remains uncertain.

    A familiar place also offers a setting for a memory palace. Personal memories retain their chosen audience; a public place model uses material cleared for its purpose. A model’s appearance does not establish that an access route, resource or service is currently available. [P1] [P2]

    Learn the next permitted task.

    Just-in-time learning brings an explanation close to the task. An authorised relief steward might find the current update procedure, watch a short maintained demonstration, practise a handover and receive feedback. The learning item identifies its equipment, procedure edition and review arrangements.

    Extended reality, or XR, offers digital information in real surroundings and immersive rehearsal. An ordinary screen, spoken explanation or practical demonstration may suit the task better. The person’s access and learning preferences guide the format.

    Viewing a recording or completing a simulation does not confer a trade qualification, emergency appointment or authority over a service. Qualified supervision and actual permissions remain part of the work.

    Enjoyable challenges create room to reflect.

    Escape Room Team Building explores independent group play, followed by reflection on language, listening, evidence, useful disagreement and repair. The current manuscript is the 30 October 2026 ROUND ONE research and calculation edition. Prices, premises, room counts, technology and operating arrangements remain open. [N2]

    The disaster connection is an opportunity to explore: engaging shared challenges could help people practise communication, problem-solving and information handover, then test whether learning carries into another setting. Screen or XR scenarios remain possible formats, rather than settled escape-room technology.

    Recording choices cover all capture channels, with workable opt-out and separate decisions about retention, personal Aura use, research and publication. Design, trades, facilitation, filming, editing and mentoring have paid scopes. C-Hours retain their voluntary, non-monetary purpose.

    The connected Teacher Training source includes outage, transport, weather, water and communications rehearsal using official sources and human roles. It offers a starting reference for discussion. Playful rehearsal builds familiarity; specialist qualifications and operational authority follow their own processes. [N3]

    Prepare for more capable intelligence.

    AI, SI, XR and robotics are explicit near-future planning concerns. Each proposed use needs a named task, available capability, chosen information, permitted action and observable result. A richer personal relationship with intelligence helps people question its suggestions and direct the work.

    A rehearsal becomes useful evidence when people explain what happened, the team records mistakes and the next edition changes in response. The AIDR Managing Exercises Handbook provides a relevant Australian reference. [R21]
