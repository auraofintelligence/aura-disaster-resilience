The club stays portable while the place stays open.

The Sandy Sports idea can learn from Amity, the Dunwich/Gumpi landing and Ballow Road site context, Point Lookout's visitor-facing town role, beach trials, grants, tenders and film pathways without pretending any site, facility or partner already exists.

The club is the people layer. Temporary beach activity can test demand across places. The longer facility horizon stays Dunwich/Gumpi, with Ballow Road treated as uncertain context inside that horizon, while Point Lookout can carry showcase, visitor, tourism-worker, retiree and media-facing moments if that role earns support.

AI assists the system. It does not become the system.

Self-sovereign does not mean doing admin alone. The stronger pattern is consent-aware records, shared access, human decision gates and AI doing the first boring draft.

Public layer

Website, join form, event calendar, waitlist, updates and public evidence.

Operations layer

Members, volunteers, money, assets, safety, rosters and recurring reminders.

Knowledge layer

Rules, policies, minutes, agreements, grant notes and handover packs.

Leadership can feel lived in.

Mixed-gender leadership with trusted female leadership changes the everyday feel of the club: who notices quiet people, who welcomes families, who calls time on rough behaviour, who handles money, who speaks in public and who younger people can imagine becoming.

The aim is a team that can stay human under pressure: visible enough to be trusted, humble enough to listen, and organised enough that no one has to carry the whole thing alone.

Self-sort without being assigned.

No one has to arrive knowing their title. A person might first be curious about playing, parenting, teaching, organising, building, health, media, funding, local business or simply making the island feel less lonely. The point is to notice where contribution feels honest now, then let that contribution mature if the club forms.

If one person is the operating system, the club is already fragile.
