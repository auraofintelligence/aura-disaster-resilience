Act four / The origin

A young ideal.

We more intended to get across we are two unlike-minded private citizens who had a conversation over a cold one, and postulated... how does one create (with the assistance of the public) a political party in Australia? This political party being truly representative of the nation as a whole, with the maximum amount of input from said nation, whilst being fully transparent and corruption proof.

Politics is already a splash zone and a shit-slinging mockery of honour until we change the system. Less splash, more class means cleaning it up, not pretending it never stank.

P4A is the front door

These are connected policy possibilities, tools and source libraries. Follow an idea into the project behind it, then use Project Atlas to explore the wider work.

  • UNGA81: Joyful Responsible Abundance — The connected proposal for capability, ownership, livelihoods, care and worthwhile futures, with a reference library.
  • C-Hour Introduction — One verified voluntary human hour equals one C-Hour, with no fixed dollar equivalent. Explore recognition, care and local choice.
  • 500 Queens 2026 — Women’s enterprise and leadership proposal, with mentoring concepts, an enterprise catalogue and original source readers.
  • Mutual Futures — A connected proposal for productive ownership, paid livelihoods, learning, care and freedom over time.
  • Project Atlas — The wider project directory: readable introductions, connected public pages and source repositories.

P4A is currently a proposed movement and drafting project. Content is exploratory unless explicitly marked as adopted policy.
