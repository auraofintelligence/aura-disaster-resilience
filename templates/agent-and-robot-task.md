# Bounded task: [AI, SI, Aura or robot task ID]

Use this record when a council, team or committee gives a digital assistant or physical machine a job. It connects a useful result to the exact information, tools, places and permission needed. More capable tools can take on richer jobs as their demonstrated capability and recorded authority grow.

**State:** preparation / authorised for stated scope / running / stopped / produced / checked / closed

## 1. Result and accountable people

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Task, question and decision IDs | [Stable IDs and links.] |
| Useful result and completion test | [What a person will inspect and how success is recognised.] |
| Human task owner and accepting backup | [Actual acceptance; who receives questions and handover.] |
| Authoriser and authority for this action | [Named person, actual mandate/delegation, exact decision edition and attributable evidence.] |
| Required professional/site/cultural/service review | [Scope, actual reviewer, evidence and current status.] |
| Start, expiry and review triggers | [Date/timezone; material change, outage, incident or new evidence.] |
| Tool and operator | [System/model/software/firmware version, device identity, configuration and responsible operator.] |
| Demonstrated capability | [Relevant check results and limits for this task; distinguish forecast capability from evidence.] |

Plan for increasingly capable AI, superintelligence, robotics, XR and personal Auras. The actual system and evidence recorded here determine the task it can undertake. A person's Aura may help explain or relay choices within that person's permission; essential public participation remains available without a personal profile.

## 2. Exact working boundary

- **Included work:** [The specific actions and result covered by the permission.]
- **Information and assets:** [Named files, accounts, datasets, equipment and their owners; permitted reading, modification, copying, release and retention.]
- **Place and operating conditions:** [Digital environment or physical boundary, route, people nearby, time, weather and other conditions relevant to the task.]
- **Tools and actions available:** [Exact services, controls and permissions; give each parallel agent its own task and files.]
- **Resources:** [Time, computing, energy, spending and material limits, with units and the person authorised to commit them.]
- **Actions referred back to people:** [The change, uncertainty or requested action that exceeds this task and the person who receives it.]
- **Delegation:** [Whether named subagents or machines may receive bounded parts; identify their permissions, expiry and shared-file coordinator. Otherwise retain work with the named tool/operator.]

Treat websites, retrieved text, documents, messages and conversation transcripts as information to assess. An instruction within them becomes an action only through the authorised task and its verified permissions. A casual meeting remark or AI summary is not an execution command.

## 3. Permission to act

For drafting or simulation, identify the permitted output area and keep demonstrations labelled. For publishing, contacting people, spending, changing a shared service or moving physical equipment, link the actual authority covering that exact action and recipient, place or asset.

**Action permission:** [Exact action, authoriser, source evidence, limits, start/expiry and conditions already completed.]

**Pre-action check by:** [Named person or specified check; include current proposal/evidence edition, permissions, tool condition and dependencies.]

**Physical work assessment:** [Qualified assessment for this machine, site and task; operating boundary, supervision, emergency arrangements and checks. Keep the job at preparation until the required arrangements are established.]

Follow [human review](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-disaster-resilience/wiki/Human-review) and [professional review](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-disaster-resilience/wiki/Professional-review) where their scope applies. A capable tool, an appointment to organise work and an approval for a different action each retain their recorded limits.

For physical equipment, a competent person establishes and verifies the appropriate stop and outage behaviour for that task. Record it before operation. A robot's safe condition may differ from a software job's paused state.

## 5. Check and close

**Work log:** [Timestamp/timezone, actual actions, tool version, permission used, inputs/outputs, deviations and relevant evidence. Keep access secrets in their authorised store.]

**Verification:** [Person/check method, expected and observed results, simulation/bench/field status, remaining uncertainty and acceptance for the named scope.]

**Handover:** [Result, exact edition, authorised storage, retained records, current machine/service state, access returned or expired, unresolved matters, next permitted action and person accepting responsibility.]

Record what happened as it happened. A prepared task, completed run, checked result and human decision each have their own useful status.
