## Prepare together

The organiser shares this agenda, a proposed [body charter](body-charter.md), a one-page kiosk example and the specific choices to discuss. Confirm the people invited, their capacity to contribute and any authority they bring. Use authorised contact routes and ask about access needs.

Ask participants how they wish to be named, recorded and assisted. Explain any transcription, AI tools or XR recording before use; agree consent, audience and retention. Offer participation through notes or a person providing access assistance. Personal Aura support is optional, with the person choosing what it may share.

## The 90-minute conversation

| Time | Conversation and practical outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 minutes | **Welcome and participation.** Introduce people, confirm access and recording choices, explain the purpose and meeting roles. Agree how to take turns, ask for clarification and contribute after the meeting. |
| 10–20 minutes | **Choose the first result.** Describe the everyday-to-disaster kiosk service in plain language. Ask what people need to understand or be able to do. Choose one result achievable in four weeks. |
| 20–35 minutes | **Map people and capability.** Invite practical knowledge, local experience, interests and available time. Map missing knowledge and relevant professional, cultural, site and service responsibilities. Record proposed participation separately from confirmed appointments. |
| 35–50 minutes | **Agree how this group works.** Work through the charter's purpose, scope, decision-makers, actual decision method and quorum, resources, record home, access, conflicts and review date. Record unresolved choices with a person preparing the next proposal. |
| 50–65 minutes | **Walk through a kiosk example.** Follow one ferry-cancellation notice from its source to a public screen, then a simulated communications/power interruption and recovery. Let people ask what a resident, visitor, operator and relief worker each need. Use a sketch, shared XR scene or paper cards with the same labelled evidence and assumptions. |
| 65–80 minutes | **Plan the first four weeks.** Give each agreed task a person who accepts it, a backup or handover, a clear result, dependencies and a realistic date. Identify bounded AI/SI assistance and the evidence needed before any physical work. |
| 80–90 minutes | **Read back and confirm.** Read exact decisions, unresolved matters and actions aloud and in accessible text. Record who actually decided each item and by which rule. Confirm the draft-minutes review route, corrections period, next meeting and the next contact point. |

If a decision needs someone who is absent or whose authority is unconfirmed, agree the evidence and proposal to bring back. Other authorised preparation can continue.

## A proposed first four weeks

Adapt dates and workload to participants. This sequence builds a worked example people can inspect before considering a field trial.

| Week | Practical work and result for review |
| --- | --- |
| 1 — Bring the group and records together | Confirm the agreed charter scope, accepted roles, record home and participation routes. Map the notice source, receiving service, site and professional reviews needed for the chosen example. Produce a labelled service sketch. |
| 2 — Build the walkthrough | Prepare an everyday notice, a replacement disruption update, accessible screen/text versions and an operator handover. Use clearly labelled sample information. Record AI tools, source editions, costs and energy assumptions. |
| 3 — Practise continuity | Plan two to three days of practical testing across three logical places and a portable unit, including stale information, relief staffing and restoration. Digital twins can simulate any chosen time span. Record simulated duration, computer runtime and measured practical results separately. |
| 4 — Review and choose the next milestone | Demonstrate the result, hear participant feedback, compare gaps with relevant practice and update the evidence. Ask the responsible people to decide the exact next scope, such as improved drawings or a separately authorised bench trial. |

## Outputs to carry forward

- The charter edition and its actual agreement status.
- A [meeting record](meeting-record.md) with attributable decisions, dissent, actions and corrections.
- The chosen kiosk result, question and decision IDs, and the source/evidence list.
- Accepted tasks with dates, resource limits and [agent or robot task records](agent-and-robot-task.md) where relevant.
- The next meeting's purpose and a handover a new participant can follow.

This meeting prepares cooperation for increasingly capable XR, AI, superintelligence, robotics and personal Auras. Demonstrate each tool's actual capabilities as it joins the work, and keep meaningful participation available through everyday devices and direct human conversation.
