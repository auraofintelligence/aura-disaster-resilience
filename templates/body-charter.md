## Participation as the tools grow

Design for in-person, phone, text and XR participation. XR means shared digital spaces or information overlaid on the physical world. Give each person an accessible way to read, contribute and confirm what they meant. A person may choose personal Aura assistance for preparation, explanation or access; a private profile is optional.

Plan for increasingly capable AI, superintelligence and robotics. Record the actual tool version, demonstrated capability and delegated task each time. People retain their current decision roles. Any future proposal for different governance receives its own review. Count a person once across their room attendance, remote connection and Aura; agree any proxy arrangement through the applicable rules.
