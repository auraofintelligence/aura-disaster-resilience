# A complete everyday-to-disaster demonstration

This first demonstration gives people something they can try, question and improve before a field pilot. Use clearly labelled sample people, notices, services and requests at three logical locations and a portable unit. Locations are exercise labels, rather than agreed hosts.

## Everyday jobs

1. A resident finds an event, checks its current status and sees the transport source and last check.
2. A visitor finds a local notice or service in a readable form and carries the information to a phone or printed alternative.
3. A person uses Aura-guided assistance or learning, chooses what personal context to share and leaves with a clear next step.

## When conditions change

An event is cancelled, a route changes and a service loses power. Show approved corrections on the available screens. Preserve the issuer, time and meaning of warnings while connections change. Show whether a request is saved locally, received, acknowledged, assigned or completed, and who owns its next action.

Divide the network into disconnected groups, restart a device and interrupt an update. Rejoin the groups and show how repeated messages, corrections and outstanding work are reconciled. A relief steward uses the offline handover, restores a replacement and continues the work.

Aura helps the steward find an approved learning item, understand it, rehearse the task, carry it out within their role and record feedback. Tasks that need qualified people connect to the confirmed qualified cover.

## Evidence to bring back

Keep a configuration list, sources and versions, test steps, observations, timings, message receipts, energy measurements where real equipment is used, access barriers and the changes people suggest. A 24-hour disconnected software exercise and a nine-day scenario timeline establish different evidence. Real hardware endurance and all-weather performance develop through their own measured tests.

The D-003 review selects the prototype service brief. Future public service, sites and construction receive their relevant people, agreements and reviews.
