# The research starting point

The kiosk design grows from Luke's original proposals, the island experience behind them and a connected body of public work. This register helps people find the source behind a question and understand what it contributes. Source documents keep their original editions, wording and rights; the wiki explains how we are using them in the current design.

## Kiosk and island preparedness

The [earlier disaster kiosk repository](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/straddie-disaster-kiosks) contains the [18-page kiosk pitch](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/straddie-disaster-kiosks/blob/main/docs/Straddie_Disaster_Kiosk_Pitch.pdf) and [15-page Sovereign Mesh presentation](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/straddie-disaster-kiosks/blob/main/docs/Minjerribah_Sovereign_Mesh.pdf). The supplied *GPT Straddie Disaster Kiosks* and *Island Resilience Sovereign Kiosks* add broader service and governance ideas.

The pitch describes hardware, communications and initial component alternatives on pages 7–10 and 14–15, with an arcade-to-outdoor prototype path on page 13. These give the physical work a starting point. The new design work brings together drawings, weather exposure, installation, energy, communications, access and maintenance.

Luke's firsthand account of Cyclone Alfred is a separate source from official records: generators were present but unconnected, qualified local cover was unavailable while barges were down, and different parts of the island experienced five to nine days without grid power. The nine-day exercise comes from that experience.

Official source routes include the [Redland local disaster plan](https://www.redland.qld.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/3/community-safety-and-health/documents/20251219-local-disaster-management-plan-control-copy.pdf), [island disaster profile](https://www.redland.qld.gov.au/Community-safety-and-health/Disasters-and-emergencies/Prepare-for-disasters/Suburb-disaster-profiles-and-risks/North-Stradbroke-Island-disaster-profile), [disaster dashboard](https://disaster.redland.qld.gov.au/), [Queensland warning guidance](https://www.disaster.qld.gov.au/watch-for-the-warnings) and [BOM warnings](https://www.bom.gov.au/weather-and-climate/warnings-and-alerts). Their current information, sharing arrangements and operating responsibilities are worked through with the relevant source owners before field use.
